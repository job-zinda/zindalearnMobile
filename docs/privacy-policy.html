Privacy Policy for ZindaLearn

Last updated: July 20, 2026

ZindaLearn ("we", "our", "us") operates the ZindaLearn mobile application (the "App"), an education platform for learning skills and career development. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you create an account or use the App, we may collect:

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number (optional), username, and profile bio.
  • Authentication data: handled via Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In. We receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google if you sign in that way.
  • Profile photo: if you choose to upload one, using your device's camera or photo gallery.
  • Course activity: courses you enroll in, your progress, and (for instructors) course content you create, which may include uploaded images.
  • Device and log data: basic technical data needed to operate the app and connect securely to our servers.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To provide and personalize the learning content and features of the App
  • To enable real-time features (such as live updates) within the App
  • To communicate with you about your account or courses
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the App

How We Store and Protect Your Information

Your authentication token is stored securely on your device using encrypted storage. Your account and course data is stored on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us operate the App, such as Firebase/Google (authentication), and as required by law.

Camera and Photo Access

The App requests camera and photo library access solely to let you upload a profile picture or, for instructors, course-related images. We do not access your camera or photos for any other purpose.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

ZindaLearn is intended for users capable of independently pursuing career and skills education and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
