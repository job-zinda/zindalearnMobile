Delete Your ZindaLearn Account

Last updated: July 24, 2026

ZindaLearn is an education app for learning skills and career development. If you'd like to delete your ZindaLearn account and its associated data, follow the steps below.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to ahmedthousiii@gmail.com from the email address associated with your ZindaLearn account.
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address or username registered on your ZindaLearn account.

We will verify your request and process the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been deleted.

What data is deleted

Once your deletion request is processed, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, username, and bio
  • Your profile photo
  • Your course enrollment and progress history
  • Any messages you sent or received within the app
  • Your authentication credentials

What data may be retained

We may retain limited information where required for legal, accounting, or security purposes (for example, records needed to comply with applicable law, or to prevent fraud and abuse). Any such data is retained only as long as necessary for that purpose and is not used for any other purpose after your account is deleted.

Contact

Questions about this process can be sent to ahmedthousiii@gmail.com.
